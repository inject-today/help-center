---
title: Registering Your Potassium Account
description: Creates your Potassium account credentials through the PSM Discord bot using your license key.
category: getting-started
appliesTo: All versions
verifiedOn: 2026-11-19
---

1. Ensure you have your **Potassium license key** ready. This is the key you received after purchasing.
**Verification:** You have the key string copied or accessible.
2. Open a **direct message** with the **PSM bot** in the Potassium Discord server.
**Verification:** A DM conversation with the PSM bot is open.
3. Send the following slash command, filling in your chosen credentials and license key:/register username: YourUsername password: YourPassword key: xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxReplace `YourUsername` with the account name you want, `YourPassword` with a secure password, and the key field with your actual license key.
**Verification:** The bot responds with a confirmation message indicating your account has been successfully registered.
4. Launch Potassium from your extracted folder (see the Downloading guide above).
**Verification:** Potassium opens and displays the login screen.
5. Enter the **username** and **password** you just registered with the bot.
**Verification:** Potassium authenticates successfully and loads the main executor interface.

> **Important:** Keep your credentials in a safe place. If you forget your password, you will need to contact support through the Discord server for recovery.

# **Still not working?**

If the bot responds with an error during registration, double-check that your license key is typed exactly as received with no extra spaces. If the key is reported as already redeemed, it may have been registered to a different Discord account.
