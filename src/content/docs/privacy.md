**Effective November 11, 2026**

Inject.Today ("we", "us") runs inject.today (the "site"). Short version: you don't need an account to read the site, we don't set tracking cookies, and we don't run advertising trackers. We count visits in a way that doesn't identify you.

## When you read the site

- **No accounts, no tracking cookies.** We don't ask who you are, and we don't set cookies. Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser and never sent to us.
- **Hosting.** The site is hosted by [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). Like any web host, Cloudflare processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, so it can deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Cloudflare may keep this information in its logs, and may set a strictly necessary security cookie to tell people apart from bots, as described in its privacy policy.
- **Visit statistics.** We use [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/) to see which pages people read and how quickly they load. It doesn't use cookies or other storage in your browser, and doesn't fingerprint you. It records the page you visited, the page that linked you to it, your browser, operating system and device type, your country (worked out from your IP address) and how long the page took to load. We only ever see totals, never individual visitors.
- **Search.** Search runs entirely in your browser. What you type isn't sent to us.
- **Product status.** The status shown for each product comes from [WEAO](https://weao.gg). Our server fetches it, so your browser only talks to inject.today and WEAO doesn't learn about your visit.

## When you contribute

- **GitHub.** Contributions, fix reports and the discussions around them happen on [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement), using your GitHub account. They are public, along with your GitHub username.
- **The guide editor.** When you sign in to the editor at `/admin`, you sign in with GitHub through a small sign-in service we run on Cloudflare. It passes GitHub's sign-in token to your browser and doesn't store it. The token stays in your browser until you sign out.

## When you email us

If you email [info@inject.today](mailto:info@inject.today), we keep your message and email address so we can answer you, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

## Other websites

The site links to product websites and Discord servers that we don't control. Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

## Children

The site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Contributing requires a GitHub account, which requires you to be at least 13.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Since we hold almost none, most requests are simple: email [info@inject.today](mailto:info@inject.today).

If you want a contribution of yours removed, we can remove it from the site. Copies may remain in the public history of the GitHub repository and in copies other people have made.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, for example by adding a new service, we'll update this page and the date at the top first.
